About Us

Ever-New Trading Ltd supplies goods across five sectors, working as the link between buyers and dependable suppliers.

We are a general trading company dealing in chemicals and petroleum products, interior decoration, food, steel and metals, and renewable energy.

We work from the requirement: understanding what the client needs, sourcing the right product and managing the order through to delivery. Clients deal with one team throughout, whether the order is a single consignment or a regular supply.

How We Work

From enquiry to delivery

Every order follows the same four steps, so clients always know where things stand.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You tell us what you need: product, specification, quantity and destination.

  2. 02

    Sourcing

    We identify suitable suppliers and obtain quotations that fit your requirement.

  3. 03

    Quality and Compliance

    Supplier checks and the documentation required for each product and route.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Shipping, logistics and handover to the agreed destination.
